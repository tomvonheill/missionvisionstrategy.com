Services

What we do.

Four ways we help mission-driven organizations get clear and get moving. Most engagements combine two or more.

Organizational assessment

A clear, evidence-based picture of how your organization is working today: what's strong, what's strained, and what to do about it.

  • Governance, structure, and decision-making review
  • Volunteer, staff, and workforce capacity
  • Financial and operational performance
  • Culture and climate assessments, including accreditation self-studies
  • A written report with prioritized recommendations for leadership

Strategic planning

A direction your board and team believe in, and a roadmap specific enough to run.

  • Facilitated planning with boards, leadership, and staff
  • Mission and vision refinement
  • Goals, priorities, and measures of success
  • Growth strategy for membership, programs, and events
  • Implementation roadmap with owners and timelines

Operations & workflow design

Processes and structures that make good work easier to do, and easier to repeat.

  • Workflow analysis and operational redesign
  • Role clarity and team structure
  • Standardized rubrics and decision frameworks
  • AI-assisted workflows, designed around people rather than replacing them
  • Content and production frameworks for media teams

Stakeholder research

Listening done well: turning what students, members, staff, and audiences tell you into better programs and decisions.

  • Stakeholder interviews and listening sessions
  • Surveys and qualitative analysis
  • User-centered program design
  • Repeatable engagement frameworks your team can keep running

Not sure where to start?

Most conversations begin with a simple question: what's getting in the way? We'll help you figure out the right first step.
